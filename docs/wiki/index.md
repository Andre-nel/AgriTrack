## Purpose

This wiki stores farm knowledge, operating notes, draft protocols and unresolved questions in Markdown files kept in Git.

## Operating Principles

- Markdown files are the source of truth.
- Veterinary and chemical treatment notes stay draft until checked against the current product label and, where appropriate, reviewed by the veterinarian.
- Farm observations must stay visibly separate from approved procedures and external references.
- Missing information is recorded as TODO lines instead of being guessed.
